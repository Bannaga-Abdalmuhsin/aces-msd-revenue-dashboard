CHANGE “UNINVOICED” TO “UNBILLED REVENUE”

Replace every occurrence of:

* Uninvoiced
* Uninvoiced Revenue

With:

* Unbilled Revenue

Do not use “Unbuilt,” because the correct accounting and financial term is “Unbilled Revenue.”

DEFINITION

Unbilled Revenue represents revenue already recognized for work completed but not yet submitted to the customer through an invoice.

In the ACES MSD dashboard:

* Revenue = Value of completed and recognized work.
* Invoiced = Value formally billed to the customer.
* Unbilled Revenue = Recognized Revenue not yet invoiced.

FORMULA

Calculate:

```text
Unbilled Revenue = Maximum of (Revenue − Invoiced, 0)
```

Code example:

```ts
const unbilledRevenue = Math.max(totalRevenue - totalInvoiced, 0);
```

Do not calculate it as:

* Work Order minus Invoiced
* Revenue minus Collected
* Invoiced minus Collected
* Outstanding invoices

These figures have different meanings.

DISPLAY REQUIREMENTS

KPI title:

`UNBILLED REVENUE`

KPI subtitle:

`Recognized revenue not yet invoiced`

Tooltip:

`Revenue recognized from completed work that has not yet been submitted to the customer as an invoice.`

Use the full value:

`SAR 2,650,211.7700`

Do not use K, M, or B abbreviations.

COLOR

Use ACES red when Unbilled Revenue is greater than zero because it requires invoicing attention.

Use muted grey when its value is zero.

Do not use green.

FILTER BEHAVIOR

The Unbilled Revenue KPI must respond to:

* Project filter
* Year filter
* Month filter
* From date
* To date

Calculate it from all filtered CSV records:

```text
MAX(SUM(filtered Revenue) − SUM(filtered Invoiced), 0)
```

Do not calculate the maximum separately for every row and then sum it, because that could produce a different total.
