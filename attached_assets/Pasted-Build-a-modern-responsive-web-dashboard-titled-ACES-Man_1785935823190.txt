Build a modern, responsive web dashboard titled:

“ACES Managed Services Department – Project Revenue Dashboard”

PURPOSE

The dashboard must import and visualize the ACES Managed Services Department’s Excel revenue sheet. It should provide management with a comprehensive view of project revenue, invoicing, collections, outstanding payments, deductions, penalties, and financial performance.

DATA SOURCE

Allow users to:

1. Upload an Excel or CSV file.
2. Validate and preview imported records before saving.
3. Map columns automatically using these exact headers:

   * Project
   * Date
   * Work Order
   * Revenue
   * Deductible
   * Invoiced
   * Invoice Date
   * Invoice No
   * Due Date
   * Collected
   * Collected Date
   * Days
   * Penalties
   * Net Revenue
4. Add, edit, or delete records manually.
5. Export filtered data and reports to Excel and PDF.
6. Retain historical uploaded data without creating duplicate records.

COLUMN DEFINITIONS AND BUSINESS RULES

1. Project

   * Name of the ongoing, completed, or closed project.
   * Add a separate project status field:

     * Ongoing
     * Completed
     * Closed

2. Date

   * This is the revenue recognition month.
   * The imported Excel value may be a complete date such as 10/30/2023.
   * Display it throughout the dashboard as “Oct 2023”.
   * Use this field for monthly and annual revenue analysis.
   * Do not confuse it with Invoice Date or Collected Date.

3. Work Order

   * Represents the expected monthly contractual value.
   * Example: A SAR 36,000,000 contract running for 36 months has a monthly Work Order value of SAR 1,000,000.

4. Revenue

   * Revenue earned based on completed work or approved Bill of Deliverables (BOD).

5. Deductible

   * Value of incomplete or undelivered work included in the Work Order.
   * Calculate the operational gap as:
     Work Order − Revenue
   * Do not automatically replace the imported Deductible value.
   * Show a warning when the imported Deductible differs materially from Work Order − Revenue.

6. Invoiced

   * Amount officially submitted to the customer for payment.

7. Invoice Date

   * Exact date on which the invoice was submitted.
   * Display as DD/MM/YYYY.
   * Keep it separate from the monthly revenue Date.

8. Invoice No

   * Unique invoice reference number.
   * Prevent duplicate invoice numbers unless the user confirms that the duplication is intentional.

9. Due Date

   * Expected payment date.
   * Default calculation:
     Invoice Date + 60 calendar days
   * If the invoice is fully collected, the Due Date may be blank in the imported sheet.
   * Preserve imported due dates when provided.
   * Do not automatically clear historical due dates unless requested.
   * Use the Collected amount to determine payment status.

10. Collected

    * Amount actually received in the company’s bank account.

11. Collected Date

    * Exact date the payment was received.
    * Display as DD/MM/YYYY.

12. Days

    * For collected invoices:
      Collected Date − Invoice Date
    * For unpaid or partially paid invoices, provide a separate live “Outstanding Age”:
      Current Date − Invoice Date
    * Do not overwrite an imported Days value without confirmation.

13. Penalties

    * Amount deducted from the invoice as a customer penalty.

14. Net Revenue

    * Net amount received after penalties.
    * Default validation formula:
      Collected − Penalties
    * Preserve imported Net Revenue values.
    * Highlight any difference between the imported amount and the calculated value.

PAYMENT STATUS LOGIC

Determine invoice status automatically:

* Not Invoiced:
  Invoiced is zero or empty.

* Invoiced – Not Due:
  Invoiced is greater than zero, Collected is zero, and Due Date is today or later.

* Due Soon:
  Outstanding invoice is due within the next 15 days.

* Overdue:
  Collected is less than Invoiced and Due Date is before today.

* Partially Collected:
  Collected is greater than zero but less than Invoiced.

* Fully Collected:
  Collected is equal to or greater than Invoiced.

* Collected Late:
  Fully collected but Collected Date is after Due Date.

* Collected on Time:
  Fully collected on or before Due Date.

For financial comparisons, use a small tolerance of SAR 1 to avoid rounding errors.

FORMULAS

* Outstanding Amount = Maximum of (Invoiced − Collected, 0)
* Overdue Amount = Outstanding Amount where Due Date is before today
* Revenue Achievement Rate = Revenue ÷ Work Order × 100
* Deductible Rate = Deductible ÷ Work Order × 100
* Invoice Conversion Rate = Invoiced ÷ Revenue × 100
* Collection Rate = Collected ÷ Invoiced × 100
* Penalty Rate = Penalties ÷ Invoiced × 100
* Net Collection Rate = Net Revenue ÷ Invoiced × 100
* Average Collection Days = Average Days for collected invoices only

Handle zero and blank denominators safely. Never show “Infinity”, “NaN”, or calculation errors.
