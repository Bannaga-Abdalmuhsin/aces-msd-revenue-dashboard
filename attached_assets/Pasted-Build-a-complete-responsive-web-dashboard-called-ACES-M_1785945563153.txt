* Deductible

Interpret them as follows:

* Project: Project name.
* Date: Revenue reporting month.
* Work Order: Expected revenue for the reporting month.
* Revenue: Actual recognized revenue based on completed work.
* Deductible: Value associated with incomplete work.

* Invoice No: Invoice reference.
* Due Date: Expected collection date.
* Collected: Amount received.

* Days: Difference between Invoice Date and Collected Date.

* Net Revenue: Net amount received.

Display the revenue Date as MMM YYYY.

Example:

10/30/2023 → Oct 2023

Display exact invoice and collection dates as DD/MM/YYYY only in the underlying data, not on the main executive dashboard.

8. FINANCIAL CALCULATIONS

Use these formulas:

Outstanding:

Maximum of (Invoiced − Collected, 0)

Expected Revenue for a selected month:

Sum of Expected Monthly Revenue for projects active during that month

Planned Revenue to Date:

Expected Monthly Revenue × eligible active reporting months

Revenue Achievement:

Actual Revenue ÷ Planned Revenue to Date × 100

Collection Rate:

Collected ÷ Invoiced × 100

Remaining PO:

Maximum of (PO Value − cumulative Actual Revenue, 0)

Net Revenue:

Use the Net Revenue value supplied by the Excel data.

Rules:

* Expected revenue applies only during the project’s active period.
* Do not calculate expected revenue before Start Date.
* Do not calculate expected revenue after End Date.
* Keep baseline records separate from imported actual records.
* Do not overwrite imported financial values.
* Do not calculate deductions, penalties, or net revenue when the required source values are blank.
* Handle blank or zero denominators safely.
* Never display NaN, Infinity, or calculation errors.
* Use decimal-safe financial handling, not normal floating-point arithmetic.
